The Safari Travel difference

We are the people actually going with you

Specialists, not agents

Our team has guided in Kenya, Tanzania, Botswana, Uganda and Zimbabwe. When we say a camp is worth the drive, we have slept there.

Tailor-made from day one

No copy-pasted packages. Your itinerary is built around your pace, your interests and the season the animals are actually active.

Small groups only

Six guests per vehicle, maximum. It is the single biggest factor in how good a safari feels, and we will not put a seventh seat in.

Priced honestly

One quote, itemised, with park fees and conservancy levies shown separately. What you see is what you pay.

Photographer-friendly

We plan around golden hour, allow beanbags and trackers, and know which camps hire out serious photographic vehicles.

Support that does not stop

A WhatsApp line to a real person in-region, at any hour, from the moment you land until the day you fly home.

How it works

Four steps, then you are on a plane

No back-and-forth over email attachments. One form, one specialist, one clear proposal.

Tell us the shape of it

Dates, budget, who is travelling, and what would make the trip genuinely memorable.

A specialist picks it up

Usually the same day. Not a queue — a named person who has done the trip.

You get a real proposal

Day-by-day, itemised, with alternatives where the price or season does not work.

We handle the rest

Flights, transfers, park fees, visas. One contact, one invoice, no surprises.
